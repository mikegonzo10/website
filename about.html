About Me

A little about who I am and what drives me.

Hey, I'm Mike Corona-Gonzalez — a cybersecurity student passionate about understanding how systems can be attacked and, more importantly, how to defend them.

My journey into security started with a fascination for how things work under the hood. That curiosity led me to CTF competitions, home lab setups, and a deep dive into networking fundamentals. Every vulnerability I study makes me a better future defender.

When I'm not hacking away in a lab or completing TryHackMe rooms, you'll find me reading threat intelligence reports, contributing to security communities, and working toward my next certification.
